# Proxim website redesign

## Direction
Rebuild the site's visual system around the reference's bold editorial studio language: oversized sans-serif typography, high-contrast brand panels, generous open space, crisp rules, and purposeful asymmetry. Keep Proxim's established cream, charcoal, and restrained orange palette, familiar brand assets, and readable content.

## Scope
- Rework the shared header and footer to establish the new visual identity across every route.
- Redesign the homepage as a striking editorial front page with a full-width opening statement, purposeful brand imagery, clear audience paths, and distinct proof / process / conversion sections.
- Restyle App Partners, Business Clients, Pricing, About, Blog, Blog Post, and Contact to share the new typography, spacing, dividers, image treatment, and CTA language while retaining their content and working controls.
- Keep the existing full-width layout, business-client restaurant image, page navigation, campaign builder, revenue calculator, pricing data, contact form, and ILIOS Digital attribution.
- Remove visual clutter and generic card-heavy, gradient, and excessive animation treatments; preserve restrained transitions where useful.

## Technical approach
- Keep the existing React routes and page-specific behavior; focus changes on presentation and shared page framing.
- Establish a small set of reusable editorial section styles via semantic global tokens and shared layout patterns.
- Verify the homepage and representative internal routes at desktop and mobile widths, then check the preview build log.
